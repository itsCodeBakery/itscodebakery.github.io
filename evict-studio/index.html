Every pixel has a provenance.

Bring an anonymized CT image and its corresponding binary lesion mask. Explore what the mask actually contains. No simulated model predictions.

Responsible interpretation

Powerful visuals. Clear limits.

A single slice is not a full examination

Lesion counts refer only to pixels in a supplied segmentation mask; image-grid percentages are not whole-lung burden. We cannot establish volume, anatomy, laterality, clinical diagnosis, or patient-level severity.

Research interface, not medical software

No image-only segmentation or clinical VLM is running on this free website. Use anonymized public research images only. A qualified radiologist must review any genuine clinical finding.
