01 / OVERVIEW

The research
question.

How can plant disease classifiers learn useful visual features with less dependence on manual labels?

Abstract / research summary

Beyond Labels explores contrastive representation learning for image-based plant disease diagnosis. The research uses augmented leaf-image views to learn a visual representation that can support downstream classification. Its focus is scalable agricultural vision, combining representation learning with disease recognition. The conference work connects the challenge of annotation effort with the need for useful visual features.
