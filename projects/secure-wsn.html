01 / OVERVIEW

The research
question.

How can intrusion detection remain effective when both digital traffic and physical-layer signals are perturbed?

Abstract / research summary

This work studies perturbation adversarial training for intrusion detection in wireless sensor networks. The framework combines projected-gradient digital attacks with Bayesian optimization of physical-layer perturbations. Mixed batch normalization separates clean and adversarial feature statistics during training. The research examines how joint exposure to these attack types can strengthen detection while retaining performance on clean inputs.

Inside the

  2. 02

    The detector learns from hybrid adversarial features, with mixed batch normalization handling different feature distributions.
