01 / OVERVIEW

The research
question.

How can a hybrid convolutional and Transformer model support accurate, interpretable diabetic retinopathy grading?

Abstract / research summary

ExViT-DR combines EfficientNet and Transformer learning for five-class diabetic retinopathy grading. The study uses APTOS 2019 retinal fundus images with contrast enhancement, training-only augmentation, and class-balanced learning. An isolated test subset measures classification performance, while Grad-CAM supports qualitative inspection of the retinal regions contributing to predictions. The work emphasizes interpretable image-level evaluation, with external clinical validation remaining a future requirement.

02 / APPROACH

Inside the
method.

  1. 01

    Prepare retinal images

    Center cropping, 224 × 224 resizing, and CLAHE contrast enhancement standardize the images.

  2. 02

    Combine local and contextual features

    EfficientNet–Transformer learning supports five-grade classification with imbalance-aware optimization.

  3. 03

    Evaluate on isolated images

    A stratified 70/15/15 split separates training, validation, and test images before augmentation.
