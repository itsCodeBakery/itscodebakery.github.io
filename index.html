About

I am a Lecturer in Computer Science and an AI researcher with a primary focus on computer vision and medical image analysis. My work explores how deep learning can produce reliable, data-efficient, and clinically meaningful visual understanding systems.

I completed my MS in Computer Science at IMSciences Peshawar with a CGPA of 3.90/4.00, graduating first in my cohort and receiving a Gold Medal. My research has included diabetic retinopathy analysis, self-supervised visual learning, and weakly supervised chest CT segmentation.

My current goal is to pursue doctoral research on robust medical AI, particularly methods that learn from imperfect annotations, model uncertainty, combine multiple modalities, and provide transparent predictions suitable for high-stakes applications.

Research Interests

My research sits at the intersection of medical imaging and trustworthy machine learning. I am especially interested in four directions:

Medical Image Analysis

Segmentation and diagnostic learning from CT, MRI, X-ray, and retinal images.

Weakly Supervised Learning

Learning reliable models from sparse scribbles, partial labels, pseudo-labels, and limited expert annotations.

Transformers & Multimodal AI

Combining local and global visual representations with clinical, language, and multimodal information.

Explainable & Trustworthy AI

Explainability, uncertainty, calibration, robustness, and risk-aware decisions for medical AI.
