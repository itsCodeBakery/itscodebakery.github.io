About

I am a computer science lecturer and AI researcher working on data-efficient and trustworthy visual intelligence. My research focuses on computer vision and medical image analysis, particularly where labels are limited, predictions must be explainable, and models need to generalize beyond curated datasets.

I earned an MS in Computer Science with a 3.9/4.0 CGPA, graduating first in a cohort of 60 and receiving the Gold Medal. I am preparing for PhD study beginning in 2027 and am interested in research groups working at the intersection of visual learning, weak supervision, multimodal models, and responsible AI.

  • Computer Vision: self-supervised learning, representation learning, segmentation, explainability, and domain generalization.
  • Medical Image Analysis: retinal disease grading, lung-lesion segmentation, sparse annotation, and uncertainty-aware learning.
  • Trustworthy Multimodal AI: vision-language models, grounded reasoning, robustness, and clinically meaningful evaluation.
I welcome conversations about PhD opportunities, research collaboration, and reproducible AI for healthcare and agriculture.
