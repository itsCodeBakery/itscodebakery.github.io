About

Research with clarity, rigor, and purpose.

I am a computer science lecturer and AI researcher focused on developing models that learn from limited labels, generalize across domains, and remain interpretable to the people who use them. My work connects methodological innovation with high-impact applications in medical imaging and plant health.

As an MS Computer Science Gold Medalist, I combine research practice with more than seven years of teaching, curriculum development, and academic technology leadership. I am especially interested in doctoral collaborations spanning trustworthy medical AI, 3D image understanding, and multimodal foundation models.
