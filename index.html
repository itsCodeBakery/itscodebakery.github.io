01 / THE RESEARCHER

About

I am Syed Shayan Ali Shah, a computer science lecturer and researcher studying deep learning and vision–language models for medical image analysis and computer vision.

My research examines how models learn visual representations, use limited annotations, and connect predictions to interpretable evidence. My projects span retinal image classification, scribble-supervised chest CT segmentation, and evidence-grounded reporting. I investigate self-supervised learning, teacher–student methods, uncertainty estimation, and the consistency between model outputs and visual evidence to understand both their capabilities and limitations.

RESEARCH FOCUS

Developing learning methods that use limited supervision effectively and make medical image predictions easier to interpret, evaluate, and connect to language.

  • Learning with limited annotations: self-supervised representation learning and teacher-guided segmentation with sparse labels.
  • Medical vision and language: retinal disease analysis, pulmonary infection segmentation, and reporting grounded in structured visual evidence.
  • Reliable intelligent systems: explainability, uncertainty-aware evaluation, and learning-based intrusion detection in networked systems.

I welcome research conversations with faculty and research groups working on medical computer vision, multimodal learning, and reliable AI. Contact me about research ↗
